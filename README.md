# Rest, Restore, Refill 🌿

30-day Christian burnout recovery journals — interactive, private, and free.
Each journal runs entirely in the browser; everything written is saved only on the
reader's own device. Nothing is collected or shared.

## What's inside every edition

- 30 daily recovery pages: scripture, reflection prompts, check-in, stress scale,
  a small recovery action, and an evening prayer & release
- Baseline burnout check-in and weekly reset scorecards
- 🌸 30 printable botanical Scripture cards
- Notes + Prayers free page
- 12 rounds per year, with Backup/Restore of entries

## Sharing a journal

Send someone the edition's link. On iPad/iPhone: open in Safari → Share → **Add to
Home Screen** to make it an app with its own icon.
